Backup & Restore

Keep a copy of your financial information so it never gets trapped on one device.

Export your data

Download a private backup file containing your budgets, goals, accounts, transactions, investments, and tax organizer information.

Your backup is a JSON file. Keep it somewhere safe, such as iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or another trusted storage location.

Restore a backup

Choose a MyMoney backup file to restore your information on this device.

Restoring replaces the current information in this browser with the backup you select. Export your current data first if you want a safety copy.

Automatic local backup

MyMoney already saves changes locally in your browser. This protects against accidental refreshes, but a downloaded backup is safer if you clear browser data, switch devices, or reinstall your browser.
Best practice: download a backup whenever you make a major change, and keep a current copy in your personal cloud storage.
